Why We Gather

Some People Still Know How to Live

Have you always wanted to grow your own food but didn't know where to start?

Are you looking for people who understand self-sufficiency, real skills, and community?

Do you feel like something important has been lost — and is worth recovering?

We are the pioneers of the homesteading lifestyle and it is our job to provide the information necessary to keep the homesteading community growing.

At Oklahoma Homestead Rendezvous, we are dedicated to creating a community of like-minded people that want to learn and teach skills to create a self-sustainable lifestyle both on and off grid.

Our rendezvous aims to give people the tools and education to create an alternative lifestyle, profit from their hard work, and to be successful at self-sufficiency.

The Experience

What You'll Find Here

Two full days of everything the homesteading community does best — learning, doing, connecting, and celebrating the life most people never find.

🎤

Dynamic Speakers

Real practitioners sharing real knowledge. Experts in homesteading, self-sufficiency, food production, and community resilience.

🔨

Skilled Demonstrations

Watch — then try it yourself. Hands-on demos covering traditional skills, modern homestead techniques, and practical craftsmanship.

💬

Real Talk Sessions

No filters, no fluff. Candid conversations about what it actually takes to build a self-sufficient life — the successes and the hard lessons.

🏪

Vendors & Marketplace

Discover tools, seeds, livestock supplies, homestead goods, and local makers. Shop from people who actually use what they sell.

🎁

Games & Giveaways

Fun for the whole family. Activities, competitions, prizes, and the kind of atmosphere that makes this more than just a conference.

🤝

Your People

Meet the community you've been looking for. Families, farmers, homesteaders, and self-reliance seekers who get it — and are building it.
